Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 (v46)

Bunnies Indonesia is still in early development. This policy explains plainly how we handle data today, and we will update it as features grow.

This page is an English guide written for English-speaking readers. If anything differs, the Indonesian original takes precedence.

Data we store

  • Account: your email address and name when you log in or sign up (taken from your email or Google account).
  • Bunnies profile (v46): your username (shown publicly as @njz_ followed by the username; it starts as the first part of your email and you can change it) and, as optional items, a bio, the year you became a Bunny, favorite song, bias member, and where you're from (country, region, city). Location is hidden by default, and you choose how much to show: country only, up to region, or up to city. Your email is never made public. You can edit or clear every item at any time, and they are deleted along with your account.
  • Saved news: the list of news items you mark with the save button. Only you can see it.
  • Listening history: when you're logged in and play songs in the Bunnies player, we store which song you played and when (song title and ID, and the time). Only you can see it, you can clear it on your account page, and it is deleted with your account. We don't record visitors who aren't logged in.
  • Login session: so you don't have to log in every time, a login token is kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage). It is removed when you log out.
  • Search terms: recorded without linking them to an account, and used only to show popular searches.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data. We don't store passwords (you log in with an email code or with Google). We don't show ads based on personal data.

Third-party services

Account data and the database are stored with Supabase. If you log in with Google, Google passes your email and profile name to us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those providers can receive visitors' IP addresses. The home and info pages have Spotify and YouTube players, which load only after you press the button (YouTube uses the youtube-nocookie.com domain). After that, Spotify or YouTube may set cookies and receive visit information under their own privacy policies.

Moderation and reports

Posts, questions, and answers reported by other users can be reviewed by the Bunnies Indonesia admin. Reports are stored together with the reporter's account and are not made public. The admin can hide or delete content that breaks the rules, and can restrict accounts that repeatedly break them. Admin actions are logged.

Your rights

On the My account page (in Indonesian) you can change your display name, download your data (profile, posts, questions, answers, and reports you sent) as a JSON file, and delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, and all content you created. Copies left in static pages disappear within about 30 minutes at most. For any other request, contact us through the Contact section.

Children

Bunnies Indonesia is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, the update date above changes too.
