A small space made
by fans, for fans

What Bunnies Indonesia is, and the promises we try to keep.

Why was it made?

There are many NewJeans fans in Indonesia, but few places where they could chat and ask questions comfortably in Indonesian. So Indonesian Bunnies built a place to share posts, questions, and comments with each other.

This English page is a separate introduction written for international fans, not a word-for-word copy of the Indonesian original, so it's easy to see what kind of place this is.

How we run the site

  • Unofficial siteNo connection to ADOR, HYBE, or NewJeans. The home page and footer say the same.
  • Only photos with permissionWe don't take photos from official sites or social media without permission. Only photos with a confirmed license are shown, with the credit and license listed.
  • Non-profitNo ads and no selling. It's only a space for fan activity.
  • Takedown requests respectedIf you tell us there's a problem with a photo or post, we check it and act quickly.

How posts and comments are moderated

Posts, questions, answers, and comments written by fans can be reported. When several reports pile up, the content is hidden automatically, and the admin then reviews it and either restores or deletes it. In your account settings you can download your data or delete your account.

How member information is handled

The member pages are based on public reports and official announcements. We don't list details like height, MBTI, or blood type, because sources disagree and they're hard to verify. Information that can change, such as a member's status in the group, comes with an as-of date.

Contact and corrections

Please send wrong information, photo requests, and any other feedback through GitHub Issues. Anything posted there is public, so please don't include personal information.
