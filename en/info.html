Timeline

Major events since debut. Official means announced by ADOR or NewJeans accounts, and Media means reported by the press, so please check the sources.

  1. July 22, 2022Debut with “Attention” Official

    The first EP, “New Jeans,” followed on August 1, 2022. Source

  2. January 2 & July 21, 2023“OMG” and the second EP, “Get Up” Official

    “Get Up” sold over a million copies. Source

  3. May 24 & June 21, 2024“How Sweet” and the Japanese debut song “Supernatural” Official

    “Supernatural” is NewJeans' latest release so far. Source

  4. November 28, 2024Members announce they are ending their contracts Media

    At a press conference they claimed ADOR had broken its contractual obligations. Source

  5. October 30, 2025Court ruling Media

    The Seoul Central District Court sided with ADOR and ordered all five members to honor their exclusive contracts through 2029. Source

  6. November 12–13, 2025Members say they will return to ADOR Media

    Haerin and Hyein first, followed by statements from Minji, Hanni, and Danielle. Source

  7. December 2025Hanni returns, Danielle's contract ends Media

    ADOR terminated only Danielle's contract, citing a loss of trust. Source

  8. July 22, 2026“2026 Summer of NewJeans” Official

    For the 4th debut anniversary, a video and photos of the four members were released on the official channels. No comeback schedule was announced with them. Source

  9. November 5–6, 2026The New York Times report on contract terms Media

    ADOR denied the report on November 6. Source (JawaPos)

More recent updates are on the News page.

How to support them the right way

Guidance for Bunnies Indonesia.

  • Listen on Spotify, Apple Music, and the official YouTube channel. Watch MVs on the official channel, not on re-uploads.
  • Before sharing news, check it with an official channel or a trustworthy outlet first. This site doesn't present rumors as fact.
  • Watch out for fake accounts and ticket or merchandise scams. Official announcements come only from the channels above.
  • Respect every member, including members who aren't currently active in the group. Don't spread personal information or attack anyone.
  • This is an unofficial fan site. Rights to photos and works belong to their owners. Please see Copyright & credits.
