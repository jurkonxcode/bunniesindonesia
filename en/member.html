Meet the NewJeans members

NewJeans debuted on July 22, 2022 under ADOR, a HYBE label, with the single “Attention.” The fandom is called “Bunnies.” In 2023, the mini album “Get Up” reached No. 1 on the US Billboard 200. The information below is as of November 9, 2026.

Kim Min-ji is the oldest member of NewJeans. The group has no official leader, but fans and the press see Minji, who is calm and mature and often acts as a mediator between the members, as the de facto leader.

Before debut she trained at Source Music, and in 2019 she appeared as the face of a HYBE global audition promotion video. Since debut she has been loved for her relaxed stage presence and warm voice.

Quick facts

  • The oldest member of NewJeans
  • Appeared in HYBE global audition promotional material in 2019
  • Appeared with the other members in the “2026 Summer of NewJeans” teaser in July 2026
Latest news

After long talks with ADOR, she appeared in an individual teaser on July 22, 2026, the group's 4th debut anniversary. It was the first moment official material clearly confirmed Minji's return.

Hanni was born and raised in Melbourne in a Vietnamese family. She was dancing with the Aemina dance crew in Melbourne when she passed the HYBE global audition held in Australia, then moved to Korea and began life as a trainee.

In 2021 she appeared in BTS's “Permission to Dance” music video, and in October 2024 she attended a National Assembly committee session and gave testimony. Fans love her soft vocals and the warmth she brings in front of the camera.

Quick facts

  • From Australia, with a Vietnamese family
  • Performed with Aemina Dance Crew in Melbourne
  • Appeared in BTS's “Permission to Dance” music video (2021)
Latest news

On December 29, 2025, ADOR announced that Hanni would resume activities. She later appeared with Minji, Haerin, and Hyein in the “2026 Summer of NewJeans” teaser.

Danielle Marsh was born in Newcastle, Australia, to a Korean mother and an Australian father. In Korea she uses the name Mo Ji-hye. She appeared on TV as a child actor from a young age, including tvN's “Rainbow Kindergarten.” After training at YG Entertainment, she passed the Source Music audition in July 2020.

She debuted in 2022 as one of the first five members of NewJeans, and is known for her bright, warm personality and her love of writing lyrics. She is credited on the lyrics of several NewJeans songs.

Quick facts

  • A founding member of NewJeans (2022)
  • Child actor on tvN's “Rainbow Kindergarten”
  • Former YG Entertainment trainee

She often makes news in the fashion world too, and has attended fashion shows for major brands.

Haerin and Hyein were the first members to say they wanted to return to ADOR (November 12, 2025). In August 2026 she appeared in Marie Claire Korea, her first ADOR project since the dispute.

Hyein Lee Hye-in

Lee Hye-in

April 21, 2008

Lee Hye-in is the youngest of NewJeans and is known as one of the tall members of the team. She started performing as a child, debuting in 2017 with the kids' group U.SSO Girl, then passed through Play With Me Club before becoming a trainee in 2021.

Since debut she has been showered with love from Bunnies as the group's youngest.

Quick facts

  • The youngest member of NewJeans
  • Formerly of the kids' groups U.SSO Girl and Play With Me Club
  • Harper's Bazaar Korea pictorial for Bottega Veneta (August 2026)
Latest news

Hyein agreed to return to ADOR together with Haerin on November 12, 2025, and took part in the “2026 Summer of NewJeans” teaser.
