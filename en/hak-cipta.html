Copyright & credits

This is a draft and has not been reviewed by a legal professional. Updated November 9, 2026.

How we handle photos and materials, and how rights holders can contact us.

This page is an English guide written for English-speaking readers. If anything differs, the Indonesian original takes precedence.

Unofficial site

Bunnies Indonesia is an unofficial fan site. It has no connection to NewJeans, ADOR, HYBE, or anyone else. Names, photos, and trademarks shown on the site belong to their respective owners.

Photos and works in the gallery

Some photos were taken from the internet and posted out of fan affection, not for sale. We don't claim ownership. Every photo gets a credit and link wherever possible, and photos without a credit are completed or removed as soon as we find the source.

Member photos (Wikimedia Commons)

The photos on the Members page come from Wikimedia Commons and are under Creative Commons licenses (CC BY 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0). They were cropped from the originals. The photographer, file link, and license are written on each member card and in the “Photo” credit. These licenses cover only the copyright in the photo, not any rights relating to the likeness of the person pictured.

Fan artists' works

Illustrations and fan art are posted only with the artist's permission, or shown only as a link to the original post.

Takedown requests

Rights holders who want a photo removed or a credit corrected, please contact us through the Contact section with the photo in question and proof of ownership. We aim to remove it within 3 business days.

Content posted by users

Users must not post material that violates other people's rights. Please see the Terms of Service.
