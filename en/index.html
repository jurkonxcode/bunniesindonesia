What is this site?

Bunnies Indonesia is an unofficial fan site run by NewJeans fans living in Indonesia. It is less a news outlet for official updates and more a small community where fans write posts, ask questions, and show their feelings through comments and reactions.

It is a space made by fans, with no connection to ADOR, HYBE, or NewJeans.

What the Indonesian fan community does

  • Fan blogFans write about favorite songs, concert experiences, and fan art. Most posts are in Indonesian.
  • Questions and answersWhen someone has just become a fan and has a question, Bunnies who joined earlier answer it. The person who asked can pick the most helpful answer.
  • Comments and reactionsEvery post has comments, and four reactions let you show how you feel: like, awesome, funny, and touching.
  • Bias chipsPick your favorite member on your profile and a small chip appears next to your name in comments.

The community pages are in Indonesian: Blog · Tanya Jawab (Q&A). Turn on your browser's translation feature to get the gist.
